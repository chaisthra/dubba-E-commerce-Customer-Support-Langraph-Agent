# Return Eligibility

## Overview

This document defines the physical and documentary conditions an item must meet to
qualify for return, separate from refund eligibility (see the Refund Eligibility
document). A return being accepted is a precondition for most refund cases that are
not damaged-in-transit or non-delivery claims.

## Required Documentation

To be eligible for return, the item must be shipped back with two things included:
the **original packaging** the product arrived in, and the **original invoice**
(either the printed slip included in the shipment or a digital copy the customer can
provide). Returns arriving without either of these will be rejected at the
warehouse inspection stage, regardless of the physical condition of the product
itself. Customers should be told to retain both the box and invoice until they are
certain they will not need to return an item.

## Physical Condition Requirements (Candles)

Because Dubba's core product line is candles, return eligibility has product-specific
physical checks:

- **Unlit**: the candle must not show any signs of having been lit. A wick with
  scorch marks, a pool of previously melted wax, or a shortened wick length compared
  to the product listing is treated as evidence the candle was used.
- **Wick length match**: the wick length on the returned item must match the wick
  length specified in the original product listing. A mismatch is treated as evidence
  of use or tampering.
- **Shape and form**: the candle must be in the same physical shape as it was
  delivered — no melting, warping, or reshaping from heat exposure (including
  accidental exposure, such as being left in a hot car or near a heat source).
- **No customer mishandling**: any damage that occurred after delivery due to the
  customer's handling (dropping, cracking the vessel, exposure to moisture) voids
  return eligibility. This is distinct from damage that occurred in transit before
  delivery, which is instead handled under the Refund Eligibility document's
  damaged-in-transit provision.

## What Disqualifies a Return

A return will be rejected if any of the following apply: the item has been lit or
burned in any capacity; the wick has been trimmed or is shorter than the listed
length; the original packaging is missing; the original invoice is missing; or the
item shows damage attributable to the customer's handling after delivery rather than
to the shipping process.

## Inspection Process

Once a returned item arrives at the warehouse, it goes through a manual inspection
against the criteria above before any associated refund is finalized. If the item
passes inspection, the return is accepted and the linked refund (if applicable)
proceeds under the Refund Eligibility document's processing timeline. If the item
fails inspection, the customer is notified of the specific reason for rejection (e.g.
"wick shows signs of use") and the item may be shipped back to the customer at their
request, or disposed of if the customer does not respond within a reasonable window.

## Return Shipping

Dubba does not currently offer prepaid return shipping labels. Customers are
responsible for return shipping costs unless the return is a result of a Dubba
fulfillment error (e.g. the wrong product was shipped), in which case return shipping
is reimbursed alongside the refund.
