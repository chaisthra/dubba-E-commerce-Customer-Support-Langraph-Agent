# Account Suspension Appeals

## Overview

Dubba may suspend a customer account under specific circumstances related to payment
failures, suspected fraud, or terms-of-service violations. This document describes
why accounts get suspended, how a customer can appeal, and what access remains
available during an appeal.

## Reasons for Suspension

An account may be suspended for any of the following reasons: repeated failed payment
attempts (indicating a payment method issue or potential fraud), suspected fraudulent
activity (such as unusual order patterns, disputed chargebacks, or mismatched
billing/shipping information flagged by the payment processor), or violation of
Dubba's platform terms of service (such as abuse of the return policy or abusive
behavior toward support staff).

## Appeal Process

A customer whose account has been suspended may submit a written appeal through
support within **30 days** of the suspension date. Appeals submitted after this
30-day window are not guaranteed review, though support may consider them at their
discretion on a case-by-case basis. The written appeal should include an explanation
of the circumstances and, where relevant, any supporting evidence (e.g. proof of a
legitimate payment method, explanation of an order pattern).

## Review Timeline

Submitted appeals are reviewed within **5 business days**. During this review period,
the account remains in its suspended state — the review does not pause or reduce the
suspension while it is being evaluated.

## Access During Appeal Review

While an account is suspended and under appeal review, the customer **cannot place
new orders**. However, the customer **retains access to view their past order
history**, including tracking information for orders placed before the suspension.
This ensures a suspended customer can still monitor existing shipments even while
their account status is being resolved.

## Appeal Outcomes

If an appeal is **approved**, the account is reinstated immediately and the customer
regains full ordering privileges. If an appeal is **denied**, the customer may not
submit a second appeal for the same suspension event — the decision on that specific
suspension is final. A customer may still open a new account or contact support for
other unrelated issues, but the denied appeal itself cannot be reopened or resubmitted.

## Repeated Suspensions

If an account is suspended, successfully appealed and reinstated, and is
subsequently suspended again for a similar reason, this is treated as a new,
independent suspension event with its own 30-day appeal window — prior appeal history
does not carry over or count against a new suspension automatically, though repeated
suspensions may be considered as context during manual review.
