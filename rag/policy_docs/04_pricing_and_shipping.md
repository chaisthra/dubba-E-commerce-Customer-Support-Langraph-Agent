# Product Pricing & Shipping (International)

## Overview

Dubba operates as an international online store, meaning customers across multiple
countries can order the same product catalog, with pricing and shipping handled
uniformly in a single currency. This document describes how pricing, currency, and
shipping fees are structured.

## Currency

All product prices on Dubba are listed and charged in **US Dollars (USD)**,
regardless of the customer's country of residence or the currency their card is
denominated in. Any currency conversion required for the customer's payment method is
handled by the customer's bank or card provider at the time of charge, not by Dubba.
Dubba does not display or accept prices in any currency other than USD.

## Product Pricing Range

Dubba's core product line — candles — ranges in price from **$20 to $50 USD**,
depending on the size of the candle and the complexity of the scent blend (single-note
scents are priced lower; layered or specialty scent blends are priced higher). Prices
are fixed per product listing and do not vary by customer location.

## Shipping Fee

A flat international shipping fee of **$5 USD** is applied to every order, regardless
of the size of the order (number of items) or the destination country. This is a
single per-order fee, not a per-item fee — a customer ordering multiple candles in one
order still pays only $5 in shipping for that order.

## Shipping Fee Refundability

The $5 shipping fee is generally **non-refundable**. The one exception is when an
order qualifies for a full refund under the Refund Eligibility document (for example,
a non-delivery case verified with the carrier, or a damaged-in-transit item) — in
those cases, the shipping fee is refunded along with the product cost, since the
customer did not receive usable value from the shipment. Shipping fees are not
refunded for standard returns where the customer changed their mind or the item did
not meet expectations but was delivered in good condition.

## Total Order Cost Example

For a customer ordering a single $35 candle, the total charge would be $35 (product)
+ $5 (shipping) = $40 USD, charged in full in USD regardless of the customer's home
currency.
