# Subscription Cancellation

## Overview

Dubba offers a recurring candle subscription option, where customers receive a new
candle shipment on a regular billing cycle. This document describes how cancellation
works, when it takes effect, and how it interacts with shipments already in progress.

## How to Cancel

Customers on a recurring candle subscription may cancel their subscription **at any
time** by going to their account settings and selecting the cancellation option.
There is no minimum commitment period, no cancellation fee, and no requirement to
contact support to cancel — it is fully self-service.

## When Cancellation Takes Effect

Cancellation takes effect at the **end of the current billing cycle**, not
immediately upon request. This means if a customer cancels partway through a billing
cycle they have already paid for, they retain subscription benefits (including any
shipment scheduled within that cycle) through the end of that cycle. No partial-cycle
refunds are issued for early cancellation — the customer is not refunded a prorated
amount for the unused portion of the current cycle.

## Shipments Already In Progress

If a subscription shipment has **already shipped** before the cancellation request is
processed, that shipment is still delivered to the customer and billed normally as
part of the cycle it belongs to. Cancellation only prevents **future** billing cycles
and shipments from occurring — it does not recall, refund, or cancel a shipment that
is already in transit or has already been charged.

## Reactivating a Cancelled Subscription

A customer who has cancelled their subscription may reactivate it at any time by
opting back in through their account settings. Reactivation starts a new billing
cycle from the date of reactivation; it does not resume or restore the previous
cycle's schedule or pricing if pricing has changed since cancellation.

## Subscription Orders and Standard Order Policies

Individual shipments within a subscription are still subject to the same Return
Eligibility, Refund Eligibility, and Shipping Delay Compensation policies as
one-time orders. Cancelling the subscription itself does not affect a customer's
ability to return, refund, or claim delay compensation on a specific shipment already
received under that subscription.
