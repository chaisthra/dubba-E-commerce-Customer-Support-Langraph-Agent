# Shipping Delay Compensation

## Overview

Dubba compensates customers automatically when a shipment takes longer than the
expected delivery window, without requiring the customer to file a formal complaint
or return request. This document defines the delay thresholds, compensation amounts,
and how domestic and international orders are treated differently.

## Domestic Delay Threshold

For domestic orders, if the order is delivered **more than 8 days** after the
shipping confirmation date (the date the carrier marks the package as shipped, not
the order date), the customer is entitled to **$4 USD** in compensation. This is
issued as either a credit to the customer's Dubba account balance or a refund to
their original payment method, depending on customer preference at the time of
resolution; if no preference is given, it defaults to an account credit.

## International Delay Threshold

Because international shipments inherently take longer due to customs processing and
longer transit distances, the delay threshold before compensation applies is extended
to **10 days** from the shipping confirmation date for international orders. The
compensation amount is the same $4 USD regardless of whether the order is domestic or
international — only the qualifying delay window differs.

## Automatic Detection and Issuance

Shipping delay compensation is designed to be issued automatically once a delayed
delivery is detected in the system — it does not require the customer to submit a
separate request, return, or refund claim. The system compares the shipping
confirmation date against the actual delivery confirmation date from the carrier; if
the gap exceeds the applicable threshold (8 days domestic, 10 days international),
compensation is triggered without manual review.

## Interaction With Refunds and Returns

Shipping delay compensation is not a substitute for a refund. A customer who
experiences a shipping delay is still entitled to the $4 USD compensation even if
they ultimately keep the item and are satisfied with it. If the customer additionally
wants to return or refund the item for unrelated reasons (e.g. they no longer want
it, or it arrived damaged), those requests are evaluated separately under the Refund
Eligibility and Return Eligibility documents. The two forms of resolution are not
mutually exclusive — a customer can receive both delay compensation and a refund on
the same order if both sets of criteria are independently met.

## Orders Not Yet Delivered

If an order has not been delivered at all and has exceeded the delay threshold, the
customer is entitled to the delay compensation regardless of final delivery outcome,
and separately may pursue a non-delivery refund under the Refund Eligibility document
if the order continues to not arrive after a reasonable additional period or after
carrier verification confirms non-delivery.
